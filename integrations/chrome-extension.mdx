## Overview

The MoltSets for LinkedIn Chrome Extension puts MoltSets enrichment in a side panel next to LinkedIn. Open a profile, click a lookup, and get verified contact data from the MoltSets database in seconds. Save the people you want into lead lists, then download them as a CSV or send them to HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Clay, Make, n8n, Zapier, or any webhook without leaving the page.

It's built for salespeople, recruiters, and founders who already prospect on LinkedIn and want accurate contact details in the tools they already use, without copying, pasting, or switching tabs.

<Card title="Install from the Chrome Web Store" icon="chrome" href="https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/moltsets-for-linkedin/bfhhpbdldppenlgnebjfpabiiiocfahp">
  Add MoltSets for LinkedIn to Chrome.
</Card>

<Note>
  The extension doesn't scrape LinkedIn. It reads only the URL of the profile in your active tab, then looks that URL up in the MoltSets database using the MoltSets API.
</Note>

## What you get

| Lookup | What it returns | API endpoint |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Full business profile** | Name, headline, job title, seniority, functional area, location, time in role, skills, and company details (name, website, LinkedIn page, industry, employee count, revenue range). Includes the business email, plus personal emails if your plan includes them. | `reverse_linkedin_lookup` |
| **Business email** | The work email only, with its risk score and last validation date. | `linkedin_to_business_email` |
| **Personal email** | A personal inbox instead of a work one. US contacts only. | `linkedin_to_best_personal_email_usa` |
| **Mobile phone** | A carrier-verified mobile number. No landlines. | `linkedin_to_mobile_phone` |

Not sure which to pick? Start with the full business profile.

Every email comes with an A–F risk score that rates how safe the mailbox is to send to. See [Email risk scores](/moltsets-data/email-risk-scores) for what each grade means.

<Info>
  Mobile lookups need a paid plan with a phone token subscription. One phone token is used per number found. See [Getting carrier-verified mobile phones](/best-practices/getting-carrier-verified-mobile-phones).
</Info>

## Requirements

- Google Chrome 116 or later
- A MoltSets account and API key. [Sign up for free](https://app.moltsets.com/signup) to start with 1,000 API calls.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    Open the [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/moltsets-for-linkedin/bfhhpbdldppenlgnebjfpabiiiocfahp) and click **Add to Chrome**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the side panel">
    Click the MoltSets icon in your Chrome toolbar. Pin it from the extensions menu so it's always one click away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your API key">
    Click **I already have an account**, paste your API key, and click **Connect**. MoltSets keys start with `ms_`. Find or create a key in the [MoltSets app](https://app.moltsets.com/dashboard/api_keys). See [Authentication](/getting-started/authentication) for details.

    New to MoltSets? Click **Sign up for free** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow LinkedIn access">
    Click **Allow LinkedIn access** and accept the Chrome prompt. Chrome describes this as reading and changing your data on linkedin.com, but the extension only reads the address of your LinkedIn tab. Nothing on the page is read or changed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Build lead lists

Organize enriched contacts into lists as you prospect: by campaign, territory, target account, or hiring role. Lists live in your browser and never use tokens.

- **Create as many lists as you need.** Each list name must be unique and 60 characters or fewer.
- **Add a contact to one list or several.** A contact can be on any number of lists.
- **Work through one list at a time.** Open a list to see only the people on it, and search within it.
- **Download or send a whole list.** Export a list as a CSV, or send every contact on it to a connected integration in one go.
- **Deleting a list never deletes its contacts.** The contacts stay in your **Enriched contacts** list.

With a list open, the same menu has **Rename list** and **Delete list**. You can also create a list there with **New list**.

With a list open, you can:

## Send contacts to HubSpot, Salesforce, and Attio

Turn LinkedIn prospecting into CRM pipeline. Once you've enriched a profile, send the contact straight to HubSpot, Salesforce, or Attio from the side panel. No CSV imports, no copy and paste, and no tab switching.

- **Go from LinkedIn to CRM in seconds.** Find a prospect, enrich them, and add them to your CRM in the same flow.
- **No duplicates.** MoltSets checks your CRM for the person's email and the company's domain first, then updates the existing record instead of creating a new one.
- **Leads or contacts in Salesforce.** Send each person as a Lead, or as a Contact linked to an Account for their company. You choose once and can change it at any time.
- **Contacts arrive linked to their company.** Each contact is created with their company record and associated to it.
- **Risk scores travel with the email.** In Attio, every email is recorded with its A–F risk score. Salesforce and Attio never get an email graded D (do not send).
- **Use the CRM your team already runs.** Connect HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, or any combination.

### Connect your CRM

For HubSpot and Attio, you connect with an access token you create in your CRM. The extension checks the token, then stores it encrypted in your browser with your passphrase. It's never sent to MoltSets.

Salesforce doesn't need a token. The extension uses the Salesforce login already in your Chrome browser.

### Send a contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enrich a profile">
    Open a LinkedIn profile and run a lookup. HubSpot needs an email. Salesforce needs the full business profile. Attio needs the full business profile or an email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click the send icon in the profile view and choose **HubSpot**, **Salesforce**, or **Attio**. A confirmation shows when the contact lands in your CRM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The profile then shows when it was sent, with a link to the record in your CRM. If you refresh the profile and the data changes, the panel marks your CRM record as out of date. Click **Resend** to update it.

To send several contacts to HubSpot at once, select them or open a list. See [Send many contacts at once](#send-many-contacts-at-once). Salesforce and Attio contacts are sent one at a time.

### What gets sent

| | HubSpot | Salesforce | Attio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Person** | Name, job title, location, email, and mobile number | Name, job title, location, email, and mobile number, with **Lead Source** set to "MoltSets" | Name, job title, location, email, mobile number, and LinkedIn URL |
| **Company** | Name, domain, description, and employee count | Lead mode: company, website, employee count, and industry on the Lead. Contact + Account mode: an Account with name, website, employee count, and industry | Name, domain, description, LinkedIn page, and employee range |
| **Extra details** | — | LinkedIn URL and revenue range in the **Description** field | A note on the person with industry, revenue range, employee count, and each email with its risk score and validation date |
| **Existing records** | Updated with the latest data | Only empty fields are filled in. An existing Account is linked but never edited | Only empty fields are filled in, so your team's edits are never overwritten |

HubSpot gets the business email, or the personal email when there's no business email. Salesforce and Attio get the same, but skip any email graded D. Fields are only sent when MoltSets found them.

#### How Salesforce matches existing records

- **Lead mode:** If a Contact with the same email already exists, the send stops and links you to that Contact. An unconverted Lead with the same email is filled in. Otherwise, a new Lead is created.
- **Contact + Account mode:** The Account is matched by website domain, then by exact company name. If there's no match, a new Account is created. A Contact with the same email is filled in and linked to the Account. Otherwise, a new Contact is created.

Some orgs restrict fields with picklists or validation rules. If your org rejects location, industry, lead source, or description, the extension sends the contact without them and tells you what it left out. Your org's duplicate rules still apply. If they flag a record, the extension links you to the existing one.

### Change or disconnect

Open **Integrations**, choose the CRM, and click **Change token** to paste a new one. Do this if you rotate the token or change its scopes in your CRM.

Click **Disconnect** to delete the token from your browser. Records already in your CRM aren't affected.

For Salesforce, open **Integrations** and choose **Salesforce**:

- Under **Create as**, switch between **Lead** and **Contact + Account**.
- Under **Default org**, choose a different org. This option only appears when you're logged in to more than one org.
- Click **Disable Salesforce** to stop sending. The extension gives back its cookie and Salesforce access. Records already in Salesforce aren't affected.

## Send contacts to Clay, Make, n8n, Zapier, or a webhook

Paste one webhook URL and you're set. There's nothing else to configure. Every contact arrives as the same flat JSON, so you can enrich it further in Clay, route it with Zapier or Make, or run any n8n workflow, cloud or self-hosted.

- **One URL per integration.** No API keys or OAuth. The extension sends one `POST` request per contact.
- **A field mapping that never breaks.** Every field is always present, set to `null` when MoltSets has no value.
- **Test before you send anyone real.** **Send a test contact** posts a sample person, "Sam Sample", so you can map fields first.
- **Emails graded D are never sent.** The email, its risk score, and its validation date are all set to `null`.

### Connect a webhook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a webhook URL">
    Create the webhook in your tool. See the steps for each tool below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in the extension">
    Open **Integrations** and choose the tool. Paste the URL into **Webhook URL** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Accept the Chrome prompt. The permission covers only that URL's website, such as `hooks.zapier.com`, not the pages you browse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test contact">
    Click **Send a test contact**, then look for "Sam Sample" in your tool and map the fields you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Clay">
    1. In Clay, open the table you want contacts to land in, or create one.
    2. Add a **Webhook** source to the table.
    3. Copy the webhook URL Clay shows and paste it in the extension.

    Clay URLs must be on `clay.com`, such as `https://api.clay.com/v3/sources/webhook/…`.

    <Tip>
      To call MoltSets from inside a Clay table instead, use the [Clay HTTP API integration](/integrations/clay).
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Make">
    1. Create a scenario and add the **Webhooks > Custom webhook** trigger.
    2. Click **Add**, name the webhook "MoltSets", and save.
    3. Copy the webhook address and paste it in the extension.

    Make URLs look like `https://hook.eu1.make.com/…`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="n8n">
    1. Add a **Webhook** node to your workflow and set the HTTP method to **POST**.
    2. Copy the **Production URL** and paste it in the extension.
    3. **Activate** the workflow. n8n doesn't accept requests to the production URL until you do.

    n8n Cloud and self-hosted instances both work, on any domain. To test a local instance, you can use `http://localhost` or `http://127.0.0.1`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Zapier">
    1. Create a Zap. For the trigger, choose **Webhooks by Zapier** and the **Catch Hook** event.
    2. Copy the webhook URL Zapier gives you and paste it in the extension.
    3. Turn the Zap on once its steps are set up.

    Zapier URLs must be on `hooks.zapier.com`, such as `https://hooks.zapier.com/hooks/catch/…`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Webhook">
    Use any endpoint that accepts a JSON `POST` over HTTPS. Paste its URL in the extension. Each contact arrives as one request.

    `http://localhost` and `http://127.0.0.1` also work, for testing on your own machine. URLs can't include a username and password.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The extension checks the URL when you save it. Clay, Make, and Zapier URLs must belong to that service, which catches most paste mistakes. It doesn't contact the URL until you send something.

### Send a contact

On an enriched profile, click the send icon and choose the integration. A webhook can take any profile with at least one lookup result.

Like CRM sends, the profile shows when it was sent. If the data changes after you send it, you can resend it.

### Webhook payload

Each request has a `Content-Type: application/json` body. The first five fields describe the send. The rest are the contact's fields, which match the [CSV export](#csv-export) columns.

```json
{
  "event": "contact.sent",
  "source": "moltsets_chrome_extension",
  "schema_version": 1,
  "sent_at": "2026-10-20T12:00:00.000Z",
  "test": false,
  "linkedin_url": "https://www.linkedin.com/in/sample-contact",
  "full_name": "Sam Sample",
  "first_name": "Sam",
  "last_name": "Sample",
  "headline": "Head of Growth at Example Co",
  "title": "Head of Growth",
  "seniority": "Head",
  "functional_area": "Marketing",
  "current_role_start_date": "2024-03",
  "skills": "Demand generation; Lifecycle marketing",
  "interests": "B2B SaaS",
  "connection_count": 500,
  "city": "Austin",
  "state": "Texas",
  "country": "United States",
  "company_name": "Example Co",
  "company_website": "example.com",
  "company_linkedin_url": "https://www.linkedin.com/company/example-co",
  "company_description": "Example Co makes example products.",
  "company_industry": "Software Development",
  "company_employee_count": 120,
  "company_employee_range": "51-200",
  "company_revenue_range": "$10M - $20M",
  "business_email": "sam@example.com",
  "business_email_risk_score": "A",
  "business_email_validated_at": "2026-10-01",
  "personal_email": "sam.sample@example.net",
  "personal_email_risk_score": "B",
  "personal_email_validated_at": "2026-09-15",
  "mobile_phone": "+15125550100",
  "mobile_phone_validated_at": "2026-09-20",
  "first_enriched_at": "2026-10-01T12:00:00.000Z",
  "last_enriched_at": "2026-10-01T12:00:00.000Z"
}
```

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `event` | Always `contact.sent` |
| `source` | Always `moltsets_chrome_extension` |
| `schema_version` | Payload format version, currently `1` |
| `sent_at` | When the contact was sent, in ISO 8601 |
| `test` | `true` for **Send a test contact**, `false` for real contacts. Filter on it to keep test data out of your workflow |

Requests are sent without cookies and time out after 15 seconds. Any `2xx` response counts as success. The extension doesn't read the response body, and it can't add authentication headers. If your endpoint needs authentication, use a secret in the URL path or query string instead.

### Change or disconnect a webhook

Open **Integrations** and choose the tool. The page shows a shortened form of the URL and the date you connected. The full URL is never shown again.

- Click **Change URL** to paste a new one.
- Click **Disconnect** to delete the URL from your browser. The extension also gives back permission to reach that website, unless another connected webhook uses it. Contacts already sent aren't affected.

## Send many contacts at once

Send a selection of contacts, or a whole list, to HubSpot or any webhook integration. Salesforce and Attio contacts are sent one at a time from each profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the contacts">
    In **Enriched contacts**, tick the contacts you want, or open a list to send everyone on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send them">
    Click the send icon in the selection bar, or next to the search box for a whole list. Choose the integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the progress">
    A progress bar shows how many have been sent and any failures so far. Keep the panel open until it's done. Click **Stop** to stop after the current contact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Contacts are sent one at a time. Contacts the integration can't take, such as HubSpot contacts with no email, are skipped. If the service rate limits a send, the extension waits 10 seconds and tries that contact again. Errors that would fail every contact, such as a deleted webhook or a revoked token, stop the batch straight away.

When it finishes, a summary shows how many were sent, skipped, and failed.

## Control what you spend

- **Nothing runs automatically.** Every lookup starts with your click.
- **Lookups that find nothing are free.** No tokens are charged for a `not_found` result.
- **Saved results don't cost again.** Results are saved in your browser. Return to a profile you've already enriched and your data is waiting. Click the refresh icon when you want the latest data.
- **Lists, CSVs, and integrations are free.** Adding contacts to lists, downloading CSVs, and sending to your integrations never use tokens.

### Track your usage

Your usage appears at the top of the panel:

- **Free plan:** the number of free tokens remaining. Each lookup that finds something uses 1 token.
- **Paid plans:** your fair use for the last 5 hours and the last week (5h records, 1w records, 5h requests). See the [Fair Use Policy](https://moltsets.com/fair-use-policy-enrichment/) and [Rate limits](/getting-started/rate-limits).

Click your account name in the footer to open the **Account** panel. It shows your plan, phone token balance, and when your limits reset. It updates as you browse, so an upgrade shows up straight away.

## Enriched contacts

Every profile you enrich lands in your **Enriched contacts** list, most recent first, with what was found for each person. Organize them further with [lead lists](#build-lead-lists).

- **Search** by name, company, or email.
- **Download** a single profile from its page, the contacts you've selected, a list, or everyone, as a CSV file.
- **Delete** profiles one at a time, or select several and delete them in bulk. Deleting a profile also takes it off every list.

The enriched contacts list reads from your browser only. Opening it never calls the API.

### CSV export

CSV files open cleanly in Excel and Google Sheets and are ready to import into any CRM. Use HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, or a workflow tool? [Send contacts directly](#send-contacts-to-your-crm-and-workflows) instead. Each file has one row per profile with these columns:

| Group | Columns |
|-------|---------|
| Person | `linkedin_url`, `full_name`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `headline`, `title`, `seniority`, `functional_area`, `current_role_start_date`, `skills`, `interests`, `connection_count` |
| Location | `city`, `state`, `country` |
| Company | `company_name`, `company_website`, `company_linkedin_url`, `company_description`, `company_industry`, `company_employee_count`, `company_employee_range`, `company_revenue_range` |
| Business email | `business_email`, `business_email_risk_score`, `business_email_validated_at` |
| Personal email | `personal_email`, `personal_email_risk_score`, `personal_email_validated_at` |
| Mobile | `mobile_phone`, `mobile_phone_validated_at` |
| History | `first_enriched_at`, `last_enriched_at` |

List fields such as `skills` and `interests` are joined with `; `.

## Privacy and permissions

- **Reads only the URL.** The extension reads the address of your active LinkedIn tab so it knows which profile you're viewing. It doesn't read page content or add anything to LinkedIn pages.
- **Sends data only when you click.** A lookup sends the profile URL and your API key to `api.moltsets.com`. Nothing is sent until you click.
- **Stores data locally.** Your API key, saved results, lists, CRM tokens, and webhook URLs stay in your browser's local extension storage. Your API key, tokens, and webhook URLs are encrypted with your passphrase. Nothing is synced to other devices or to your MoltSets account.
- **Sends to your integrations only when you click.** HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, and webhooks are contacted only if you connect them, and contacts are sent only when you click send. They go straight from your browser to the service, never through MoltSets.
- **Never stores your Salesforce login.** The extension reads your Salesforce session cookie fresh for each request. It's never saved, logged, or sent to MoltSets.
- **No tracking.** The extension contains no analytics or advertising code.

Permissions the extension requests:

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| Storage | Keeps your API key, saved results, and lists on your device |
| Side panel | Shows MoltSets beside the page you're viewing |
| `linkedin.com` | Reads the address of your active tab so the panel can tell when you're on a profile |
| `api.moltsets.com` | Runs the lookups you ask for |
| `api.hubapi.com` (optional) | Checks your HubSpot token and sends contacts to HubSpot. Requested only when you connect HubSpot |
| `api.attio.com` (optional) | Checks your Attio token and sends contacts to Attio. Requested only when you connect Attio |
| Cookies, `salesforce.com`, and `force.com` (optional) | Uses the Salesforce login in your browser to find your org and send contacts to it. Only the Salesforce session cookie is read. Requested only when you enable Salesforce, and given back when you disable it |
| Your webhook's website (optional) | Sends contacts to the webhook URL you save. Requested only for that one website when you save the URL, and given back when you disconnect. Never used to read websites you visit |

### Remove your data

- Click the sign-out icon in the footer to delete your API key from the browser. Saved lookups remain until you delete them.
- Delete saved lookups for one profile, selected profiles, or all profiles from the **Enriched contacts** list.
- Click **Disconnect** on an integration's page to delete its token or webhook URL. For Salesforce, click **Disable Salesforce**.
- Delete a list at any time. Its contacts stay saved.
- Remove the extension from Chrome to delete everything it has stored.

See the [MoltSets Privacy Policy](https://moltsets.com/privacy-policy) for full details.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The panel doesn't recognize the profile">
    Make sure you're on a personal LinkedIn profile page and that you clicked **Allow LinkedIn access**. Company pages, search results, and the feed aren't profiles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mobile lookups are locked">
    Mobile lookups need a paid plan and a phone token subscription. Click **View plans** in the panel or see [pricing](https://moltsets.com/pricing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I've used all my free tokens">
    Upgrade to a paid plan from the [MoltSets billing page](https://app.moltsets.com/dashboard/billing) to keep looking people up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My HubSpot or Attio token is missing scopes">
    When you connect, the extension lists the scopes your token is missing. Add them to the private app in HubSpot or the access token in Attio. Then open **Integrations**, click **Change token**, and paste the token again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The send option is unavailable">
    HubSpot needs an email for the contact. Salesforce needs the full business profile, because Salesforce requires a last name. Attio needs the full business profile or an email. Webhooks need at least one lookup result. Run one of those lookups first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The extension says my webhook URL isn't valid">
    The URL must start with `https://` and can't include a username or password. Clay, Make, and Zapier URLs must be on that service's domain. Copy the whole URL again from your tool. For any other endpoint, use the **Webhook** integration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="n8n didn't accept the contact">
    Make sure you pasted the **Production URL**, not the test URL, and that the workflow is **active**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My webhook refused the request (401 or 403)">
    The extension can't add authentication headers. Remove header authentication from the webhook, or use a secret in the URL instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The extension no longer has permission to reach my webhook">
    The permission may have been removed in `chrome://extensions`. Open the integration, click **Change URL**, and save the URL again to allow it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My Salesforce session has expired">
    The extension uses your Salesforce login, so it stops working when you log out or your session times out. Log in to Salesforce in the same Chrome browser and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Salesforce says I don't have permission or API access">
    Your Salesforce edition must include API access, and your user needs the **API Enabled** permission. Ask your Salesforce admin to check both. Orgs with **API Access Control** turned on aren't supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Salesforce says the person is already a Contact">
    In Lead mode, the extension won't create a Lead for someone who's already a Contact. Click **View in Salesforce** to open the existing Contact, or switch to **Contact + Account** to update it instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Salesforce or Attio is unavailable when I send selected contacts">
    Salesforce and Attio show **Send one contact at a time** in the bulk send menu. Open each profile and send it from there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The data looks wrong">
    Click **Report a problem** at the bottom of the profile view, or email [support@moltsets.com](mailto:support@moltsets.com).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
